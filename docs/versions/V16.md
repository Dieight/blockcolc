## Compatibility And Data

- Domain schema remains `7`; terrain generation version is a derived-world compatibility field, not task progress.
- Current schema-7 states and backups that still store terrain generation version 3 normalize to version 4 on load. Natural Valley rebuilds only its derived scenery from the stable seed; all user facts remain unchanged.
- Classic Island retains its existing geometry and only normalizes the stored compatibility version when required by validation.
- Imported resource-pack source files and normalized manifests remain schema-compatible. Generated GPU mip levels are runtime data and do not enter backups.
- Focus and break durations are read from the frozen plan/current session. Changing Settings affects the next plan and cannot rewrite an active timer.

## Non-goals

- Do not add new biomes, weather systems, terrain themes, task types, cloud services, or online generation.
- Do not make mountains compete with the maximum legal 128-block imported blueprint height.
- Do not copy Minecraft textures or third-party shader assets.
- Do not add TAA, temporal accumulation, deferred rendering, WebGPU-only behavior, or a permanent animation loop.
- Do not change the public version until the accumulated update is explicitly approved for Release. Approval was received for `v1.3.0` after the final maximum-zoom terrain-boundary fix.

## Acceptance

- A fixed multi-seed matrix is deterministic and demonstrates lower median water coverage and fewer small water components than version 3, zero protected water cells, and no uphill river step; far LOD requires sampled water coverage before emitting a full cell.
- Version-4 mountain maxima reach the agreed range on representative seeds while lowland and protected-settlement percentiles remain stable.
- Terrain geometry and generation cost stay bounded; shoreline refinement does not materially regress camera interaction on the current device baseline.
- Imported high-frequency textures remain crisp nearby and visibly steadier at far zoom without atlas bleeding. Cinematic does not lose edge antialiasing when Bloom is enabled.
- The idle timer updates for one through four rounds and uses no final break in its total. Active focus/background recovery still derives from the current session `endsAt`.
- `360x800`, `412x915`, `915x412`, `768x1024`, and `1440x900` keep the timer and primary action visible without overlap; scrollable work pages retain only intentional navigation/safe-area clearance at the end.
- The affected renderer path stays nonblank, request-on-demand, and within the established draw-call, frame-time, PSS, and thermal expectations.
- At maximum zoom, rotating mountains through the lower viewport edge must not clip, dissolve into the sky, or expose void; all orbit angles retain a positive full-terrain clip margin while the default settlement scale remains unchanged.

## Risks

- Raising mountains can expose coarse LOD silhouettes even when triangle count is unchanged.
- Atlas mipmaps add about one third of the atlas texel memory and require isolation at every mip level to avoid color bleed.
- Multisampling the full-resolution Cinematic scene target increases transient GPU memory and resolve cost; low-capability devices must keep their existing downgrade path.
- Automatically rebuilding derived Natural Valley terrain changes scenery around an existing settlement. Stable seeds make the result repeatable, but visual rollback requires restoring a pre-V16 backup/build.
