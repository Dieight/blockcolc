# Blockcolc Cloud Terms and Privacy Notice

Updated November 20, 2026

## Using the cloud service

Blockcolc works offline. Sign in to sync your records across devices, keep one merged daily backup, and choose whether to use Friends.

Use your own account, keep your password safe, and upload only blueprints and other content you have permission to use and share. Do not access other users’ records, disrupt the service, or abuse friend requests.

One device runs a focus plan. Another signed-in device can take over. Your local records remain available offline. When records diverge, choose what to merge or replace. Replacing cloud data or restoring a backup changes the shared workspace. You can export important records to a local file.

## What the service processes

We store your username, display name, time zone, salted password verifier, and hashed session tokens. Device IDs, names, and last access times support session management and plan takeover. Connection addresses are used to rate-limit requests.

Workspace sync covers tasks, focus history, notes, buildings and rewards, imported blueprints, and some preferences. The cloud keeps the current workspace and one merged recovery snapshot. Imported textures, original audio files, location, and date countdowns stay on your device.

When you enable Real weather, Blockcolc sends your location to QWeather for weather and astronomy. Turn it off to use local weather. Update checks request release information from GitHub without sending your Blockcolc account or focus history.

## Friends and the focus report

Friends is off by default. You can choose to share live status, the activity name, start and end times, or daily focus totals. Regular friends accept an invitation. Manage what they can see in Account or the friend’s details.

Sign in to the focus report to view focus data shared by the site owner. The owner can see today’s visitors’ usernames and visit times and the total visits by account. The browser uses a session cookie to keep you signed in.

## Retention and security

The account service uses HTTPS. Passwords use salted scrypt verifiers and sessions use hashed tokens. Server maintainers can access stored cloud data for service operation and recovery. Workspace sync does not provide end-to-end encryption.

Sign out or revoke a device to end its access. The synced workspace and recovery snapshot remain until replaced or deleted on request. Detailed visit times are removed the following day and totals continue to accumulate. Restricted service recovery copies may retain earlier data until they rotate.

## Managing your data

Settings lets you export local JSON, change your password, sign out, revoke devices, or disable sharing. Signing out keeps local and previously synced records. Contact the Blockcolc maintainers to request an account closure, a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. You can contact the project through its repository. Keep credentials, backups, and detailed records out of public posts.
